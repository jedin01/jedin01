What I do

From the whiteboard to production.

I work across the whole cycle of a business process: understanding it with the people who own it, modeling it, writing the code behind it and keeping it running.

Process orchestration

Executable processes on Camunda 8 and Zeebe.

  • BPMN 2.0 models with timers, error events and user tasks
  • DMN decision tables for business rules
  • FEEL expressions for mappings and conditions

Backend & integrations

The Java code behind each process step.

  • Spring Boot job workers
  • Core banking and middleware integrations
  • REST and SQL connectors, idempotent writes

Platform & operations

Self-managed environments that stay up.

  • Camunda 8 with Keycloak OIDC
  • Docker, Podman and Linux
  • Incident handling in Operate and Tasklist

Data modeling

Schemas that match the business.

  • Conceptual, logical and physical models
  • Normalization, keys and constraints
  • PostgreSQL, MSSQL and MySQL

Every step modeled, every step running.
