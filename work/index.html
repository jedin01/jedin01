How I work

Engineering for processes that can't fail.

Banking processes run every day, with real customers and real money behind them. I design them so that every step is visible, auditable and safe to retry.

Close to the business

I gather requirements with business and operations teams and turn their rules into DMN tables they can read.

Safe to retry

Idempotent writes and explicit error handling, so a retried job never leaves an inconsistent record behind.

Readable on Monday

Teaching made me write code, processes and documentation that someone else can pick up without me in the room.

Remote-ready

Based in Luanda, on the same working hours as most of Europe, and comfortable working async in English.
